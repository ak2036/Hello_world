## 🌸 Ankitha's Glow Quest

A pink, game-style tracker in [`glow-quest/index.html`](glow-quest/index.html) — one self-contained file, no install.

- **🌸 Cycle** – log flow, mood & symptoms; predicts next period, fertile window and ovulation from past cycles.
- **🍭 Sugar cut** – daily sugar-free check-in, streaks, a 30-day "Blossom Jar" and a calendar.
- **💤 Sleep** – bedtime/wake time + quality hearts, 7-night chart and good-sleep streaks.
- **🎬 Movies** – watch list, mark watched, rate with stars, and a "spin for tonight's movie" picker.
- **🎮 Game layer** – XP, levels with titles, a pet (Mochi) that evolves, daily quests, Perfect Day bonus and 15 badges.

**Use it:** open `glow-quest/index.html` in a browser (or turn on GitHub Pages and open it on her phone → "Add to Home Screen").
Data is stored only in that browser (localStorage) — use ⚙️ → Export/Import for backups.
Predictions are estimates, not medical or contraception advice.
